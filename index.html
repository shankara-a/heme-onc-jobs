Where the data comes from

A GitHub Action re-scrapes these boards daily and commits data/jobs.json; the site is static. Sources: ASCO Career Center, NEJM CareerCenter, ASH Job Center, PracticeMatch, Inside Higher Ed / Nature / Science Careers, UC Recruit (UCSF, UCLA, UCSD, UC Davis, UC Irvine), Stanford Faculty Positions, and industry career sites on Workday (Amgen, Gilead, Roche/Genentech, BMS, Pfizer, Merck, AstraZeneca, Novartis, Sanofi, GSK) and Greenhouse (Natera, Revolution Medicines, Nurix, Summit, Kymera, Erasca…). Cross-listings are merged; every card links to each board it appears on.

    How fields are derived

    • Relevance — title/description must reference hematology, oncology, leukemia/lymphoma/myeloma, BMT/cell therapy or cancer, and the title must not be a non-physician role (NP/PA, RN, pharmacist, coordinator, MSL…) or another specialty (radiation/surgical/gyn oncology, pathology…).
    • Job type — keyword scoring: academic (professor, faculty, university, tenure, UC "Clinical X / HS Clinical"), community (private practice, medical group, partnership, wRVU, hospital-employed, large community networks), industry (pharma/biotech, clinical development, medical director, pharmacovigilance), government (VA, NIH, military), locums.
    • MD/DO required — flagged when the posting references MD/DO, board certification/eligibility, or medical licensure. Postings that accept "MD or PhD" are left unflagged.
    • Salary — dollar ranges near words like salary/compensation/base; hourly and daily rates are annualized (×2080 h, ×220 d) and marked as such. Bonus, relocation and loan amounts are excluded from salary but captured under benefits. California, Colorado, New York, Washington and several other states legally require ranges in postings, which is why disclosure is highest there.
    • Clinical / research / admin split — patterns like "80% clinical", "20% protected research time", "3 days/week clinic". When only research is stated, clinical is inferred as the remainder.
    • Benefits — keyword tags (sign-on bonus, relocation, loan repayment, CME, malpractice/tail, retirement, PTO, partnership track, wRVU/productivity bonus, visa sponsorship, research start-up, 4-day week…).
    • Claude pass — each posting is also read by Claude (Sonnet) with a fixed extraction schema: job type, MD requirement, base salary, clinical / research / admin / teaching %, benefits as stated, call schedule, and a two-sentence summary. It only reports what the posting states or clearly implies. Postings with this pass show an "AI-read" tag; the raw rule-based read remains the fallback for anything not yet processed.

    All extraction is heuristic. Always confirm details with the posting itself — every card links to the original listing.
